#  FuzzyMainframes

This organization was created and maintained by a sole individual who, due to personal reasons, has been unable to continue maintaining these projects. We respect their privacy and wish them the best.

As a result, repositories across this organization have been dormant since 2023, and issues and pull requests have gone unreviewed — in some cases for nearly three years. We understand that's frustrating, especially for anyone who took the time to contribute or report problems.

A new custodian has recently taken over stewardship of the organization. The immediate priorities are:

- Reviewing the state of each repository and determining what's still relevant and viable
- Triaging the backlog of open issues and pull requests
- Archiving projects that are no longer maintained so expectations are clear

This will take time, and not every repo or PR will necessarily be revived. But the goal is transparency: making it clear what's actively maintained, what's preserved for reference, and what's been sunset.

If you've been waiting on a response, thank you for your patience.
